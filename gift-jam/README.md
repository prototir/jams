# The Gift Jam: Official Rules

![The Gift Jam](cover.png)

**Play and enter on Prototir:** https://prototir.com/jams

**No purchase or payment is necessary to enter or win.** This is a skill contest. Winners are chosen on merit, not by chance.

## 1. Organizer

Prototir (prototir.com). Contact: prototir@experir.com.

## 2. Dates

The jam opens on Saturday, October 10, 2026 and submissions close at midnight UK time at the end of Friday, October 30, 2026 (00:00 GMT on October 31, which is 01:00 CET). Entries received after that time are not judged.

## 3. Eligibility

Open to anyone who holds a Prototir account in good standing and meets the Prototir minimum age of 16, where entering is lawful. Employees and contractors of the Organizer and their household members cannot win. Void where prohibited.

## 4. How to enter

1. Sign in to Prototir (a free account is enough).
2. Publish a prototype on Prototir that follows the Prototir Terms.
3. Submit it to The Gift Jam on its jam page before the closing time.

Limit: one entry per person. The entry must be your own work, or work you have the rights to publish. Previously published prototypes are allowed. Entries that are offensive, infringe someone else's rights, or break the Prototir Terms are disqualified.

## 5. Judging

The Prototir team judges all valid entries after submissions close, on these criteria with equal weight:

- **Beauty:** visuals, sound, motion and overall feel
- **Care:** polish and attention to detail
- **Delight:** how pleasant it is to try
- **It works:** it opens and runs on Prototir without extra setup

The 10 highest-scoring entries win. Ties are broken by the Beauty score. The judges' decision is final.

## 6. Prizes

10 prizes. Each winner receives 1 year (12 months) of the Prototir Pro Plan, approximate retail value USD 50 (total USD 500). If a winner already has a paid Pro or Team Plan, it is extended by 12 months. Prizes are personal, non-transferable and cannot be exchanged for cash or any other prize. A winner can receive only one prize.

## 7. Winner notification and delivery

Winners are picked on Monday, November 2, 2026, announced on the jam page and on Prototir's X account, and notified through their Prototir account email. The plan is applied to the winner's account within 7 days. A winner who cannot be reached or does not meet these rules within 14 days forfeits the prize, and it passes to the next-ranked entry.

## 8. Taxes

Any taxes due on a prize in the winner's country are the winner's responsibility.

## 9. Rights and publicity

Entrants keep ownership of their work. By entering, you allow the Organizer to show your entry's title, screenshots, creator name and link when promoting the jam and its results.

## 10. Privacy

Personal data is processed only to run this jam and deliver prizes, as described in the Prototir Privacy Policy (prototir.com/legal/privacy).

## 11. General

The Organizer may cancel, suspend or change the jam if something outside its control prevents it from running fairly, and will announce any change on the jam page. These rules are governed by Italian law. Questions and disputes: prototir@experir.com.
